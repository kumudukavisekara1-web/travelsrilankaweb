The Pearl of the Indian Ocean

A Land Like No Other on Earth

For over two thousand years, travelers from Marco Polo to modern adventurers have been enchanted by the compact jewel that is Sri Lanka. Known in antiquity as Taprobane, Serendib, and Ceylon, this tear-drop shaped island packs a mesmerizing kaleidoscope of landscapes within a few hours’ drive.

Wake up to the roar of Indian Ocean waves, ascend through sacred cloud forests to misty tea estates, track majestic leopards and elephant herds before dusk, and dine under starlit skies on authentic Ceylon clay-pot curries.
